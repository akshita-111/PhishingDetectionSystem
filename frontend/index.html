About the model

Random Forest Classifier

An ensemble learning method that builds hundreds of decision trees and merges them for a more accurate, stable prediction. Particularly effective for URL-based threat detection due to its resistance to overfitting.

The model was trained on a balanced dataset of phishing and legitimate URLs, sourced from public repositories and real-world threat feeds.

Phishing Red Flags

  • Urgent or threatening language pressuring immediate action
  • Misspelled or look-alike domain names
  • Requests for passwords via email or link
  • No HTTPS in the address bar
  • Suspicious shortened or redirect links
