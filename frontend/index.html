⚠️ Phishing Red Flags

  • Urgent/threatening language — pressure to act immediately
  • Mismatched or misspelled domains — fake URLs that look real
  • Requests for passwords/personal info — legitimate sites don't ask via email
  • Missing HTTPS — insecure connection indicator
  • Suspicious shortened links — hover to see real URL before clicking
